arcxed@outlook.com

Tell us your device, iOS version and what happened. We usually reply within a few days.

A purchase didn't arrive

Close and reopen the game: a purchase that was interrupted is delivered the next time it starts. For Remove Ads, x2 Profit or the Starter Pack, open the GOALS tab and tap Restore Purchases. Chip packs can't be restored, so if chips didn't arrive, email us and include the date of the purchase.

New phone or reinstalled the game

Your progress is saved only on your device, so a new install starts a fresh city. Anything you bought once (Remove Ads, x2 Profit, the Starter Pack) comes back with Restore Purchases.

Refunds

Payments go through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads

  • The banner starts after you hire your first Fixer. Remove Ads turns it off for good.
  • Reward videos only play when you tap to watch one.
