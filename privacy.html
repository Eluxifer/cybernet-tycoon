CyberNet Tycoon

Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026 · made by Arcxed

The game does not collect your personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking of its own. Your progress is saved only on your device, inside the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes your save.

Advertising

The game shows a banner ad, and optional reward videos you choose to watch, served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure those ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects limited device and usage information, such as a device identifier, approximate location derived from the IP address, ad interactions and diagnostics.

Ads in this game are requested as non-personalised, and the app does not ask to track you across other companies' apps and websites. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before any ad is requested. Buying Remove Ads turns the banner off.

Google describes how it uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The game never sees your name, Apple Account or payment details. It only receives confirmation of which item was bought, so it can deliver it and restore it later. Apple's privacy policy is at apple.com/legal/privacy.

What the game never touches

Your contacts, photos, camera, microphone and precise location.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if a future version adds personalised ads or online features, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Questions: contact Arcxed through the support link on the game's App Store page.
